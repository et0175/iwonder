## What they can see

In summer the Sun climbs high and the day is long. In winter it stays low and the day is short. The Sun itself is unchanged.

## What they ask

> Where does the Sun go in the winter?

## Watch out

The distance explanation is the single most common wrong answer, and adults give it too. It is also falsifiable in one sentence: the Earth is nearest the Sun in January. A torch on a sheet of paper, straight on and then slanted, is the whole experiment.
