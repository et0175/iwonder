## What they can see

A ship sailing away does not shrink to a dot — it sinks, hull first, mast last. And the horizon keeps its distance however far you walk towards it.

## What they ask

> What happens to the boat when it goes over the edge?

## Watch out

Hull-first is the whole proof and it is easy to miss with the naked eye. A long table, a beach ball and a toy boat at eye level gives it instantly — the boat vanishes bottom-first over the ball and all-at-once off the table.
