## What they can see

Everything aboard the station floats at once — the people, the water, the crumbs, the tools. Nothing is singled out.

## What they ask

> Why do astronauts float?

## Short answer

Gravity at the space station is about 90% of its strength at ground level. Astronauts float because the station and everything in it are falling together, so nothing presses against anything. Weightlessness is the absence of support, not the absence of gravity.

## Watch out

Zero gravity is the phrase to avoid entirely. Gravity at the space station is nearly as strong as on the ground; what has gone is the floor pushing back.
