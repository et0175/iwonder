## What they can see

A train on the next platform pulls out, and for a second it is you who seems to be moving. Trees stream backwards past the car window although not one of them has moved.

## What they ask

> Why is the tree running away from us?

## Short answer

Motion only exists relative to something else. Inside the car the natural description is that the trees move; from the roadside, that the car does. Neither is more true, and because steady motion cannot be felt, no experiment performed inside the car can decide between them. Which thing you call stationary is a choice, not a discovery — and that choice is what later makes a spinning Earth thinkable.

## Watch out

This is the concept that makes the whole Space chain possible, and it is almost always skipped. A child who has not had the "which of us is moving?" moment has no reason to accept that the Sun stays put while the ground turns — they will accept the words and keep the old picture.
