## What they can see

A furnace the size of the Sun, shrunk by distance to a pinprick you can hide with a finger.

## What they ask

> Is our Sun a star as well?

## Short answer

Every star is a sun; ours looks different only because it is 270,000 times closer than the nearest other one. A star reduced to a point of light is the clearest demonstration of what distance does to apparent size.
