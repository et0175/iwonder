## What they can see

It covers no more sky than a coin held at arm's length, yet it warms an entire world at once and lights it from one side.

## What they ask

> Is the Sun small, or is it just far away?

## Short answer

The Sun appears half a degree across, the same as the Moon, but it is 400 times further away and therefore 400 times larger: about 109 Earths wide. Its apparent smallness is entirely an artefact of 150 million kilometres of distance.
