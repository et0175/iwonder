The Edge of the World

Max was lying on his stomach at the end of the pier.

Far out on the lake, a little white boat was sailing away.

“It's shrinking,” he announced.

Kira sat beside him.

“It isn't shrinking.”

“It definitely is. Look.”

The boat was already small enough to cover with Max's fingertip.

“Maybe boats shrink when they get far away,” said Strange.

Kira looked at him.

“And then grow again when they come home?”

Strange considered this.

“That would explain why boats need harbours.”

Professor Ada lowered her binoculars.

“Keep watching.”

For a while, nothing happened.

Then Kira sat up.

“The bottom's gone.”

Max grabbed the binoculars.

The white hull had disappeared. But the little mast was still there.

Then half the mast disappeared.

Then the whole boat was gone.

Max stared at the empty water.

“Where did it go?”

“Perhaps it fell off,” said Strange.

Max turned slowly towards him.

“Off what?”

“The edge.”

There was a short silence.

Then Max jumped up.

“We need a boat.”

“And an edge,” said Strange.

Back at Professor Ada's house, Max found a toy boat.

Strange found the kitchen table.

“This,” he said, “is the world.”

Professor Ada looked at her table.

“For the next five minutes.”

Max pushed the boat away from Kira.

It became smaller and smaller.

But Kira could still see the whole boat.

“Nothing disappeared from the bottom.”

Strange crouched down and examined the table suspiciously.

“Perhaps this world is the wrong shape.”

Professor Ada rolled a large exercise ball into the room.

Max put the boat on top.

Kira lowered her head until her eyes were level with the ball.

“Go.”

Max slowly moved the boat away.

The hull vanished.

Then the deck.

For one moment only the tiny mast remained.

Then—

gone.

Kira stood up.

“Again.”

They did it again.

Hull.

Deck.

Mast.

Gone.

Max looked from the ball to the window.

Then back to the ball.

“The lake isn't flat.”

Nobody answered.

He frowned.

“It looks flat.”

Kira ran her hand over the huge ball.

“So would this,” she said, “if we were tiny enough.”

Strange climbed onto a chair.

Then onto the table.

Professor Ada looked at him.

“I need to see it from higher up.”

“Not on my table.”

Strange climbed down.

“Science has many rules.”

Max was still staring at the ball.

“So there isn't an edge?”

Kira smiled.

“There is.”

Max looked alarmed.

She pointed toward the window.

“The horizon.”

Strange picked up the toy boat.

“Excellent. Let's go and find it.”

Max was already putting on his shoes.

Professor Ada sighed and reached for hers
